About Ethereal

Miralain Hospitality Group

A place born
from devotion.

Ethereal is proudly owned and operated by Miralain Hospitality Group founded by Managing Partners Nick Alain and Chef Estuardo Mira, combining world-class design, culinary excellence, and hospitality leadership.

Nick and Estuardo are joined by their wives, Elena and Natalia, in Miralain’s strategic vision, stewardship, and oversight.

Miralain first opened Glimmer Café in Delray Beach in 2025, followed by the opening of Ethereal in 2026 right across the street from Glimmer.

Nick Alain

Design & Vision

Nick Alain is an acclaimed designer known for creating extraordinary hospitality spaces that inspire emotion and connection. Nick has dedicated his lifelong career to the pursuit of art and visual design. Consistently developing and reinventing his style to what the world sees today, he has created wonders of art often described as “timeless, mechanically elegant nouveau chic.” Nick’s vision for Ethereal was to create a space that feels elegant, immersive, and unforgettable.

Chef Estuardo Mira portrait

Chef Estuardo Mira

Culinary & Operations

Chef Estuardo Mira brings decades of restaurant industry experience and culinary expertise to Ethereal. As the driving force behind successful South Florida restaurant concepts including Malbec Grill, 3:33 Burgers, and Glimmer Café, Chef Estuardo has built his reputation on quality, consistency, and exceptional hospitality.
